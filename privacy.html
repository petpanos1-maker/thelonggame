Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

The Long Game Publisher is an internal tool used exclusively by The Long Game to publish our own original video content to our own social media accounts (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok).

What we collect

Nothing from visitors. This website is a static page with no cookies, no analytics, no tracking, and no forms.

TikTok authorization

When we connect our own TikTok account through TikTok's Login Kit, TikTok provides us with an access token for our own account only. It is stored privately on our own equipment and used solely to upload our own videos to our own account. We do not access, collect, or store any data about any other TikTok user.

Data sharing
